Everything you need

Built for Serious Raiders

From live lobby feeds to boss counters, GO Raiders gives you every advantage before the battle starts.

📡
Live Lobby Feed
Browse open raid lobbies in real time. Filter by tier, boss type, or team. Pull to refresh or let it auto-update.
🎯
Boss Counters & Stats
Tap Details on any boss to see its weaknesses, best counters, moveset, and weather-boosted CP — right in the app.
⚡
Instant Hosting
Create a lobby in seconds. Set raid tier, weather boost, gym team, and capacity. Your lobby goes live immediately.
🏅
Boss Filter
Multi-select filter lets you pin specific bosses. Only see lobbies for the Pokémon you actually want to catch.
👤
Trainer Profiles
Scan your Pokémon GO profile or enter manually. Your friend code, level, and team are shared when you join lobbies.
🔔
Queue & Auto-Join
Join a boss queue and get notified the moment a matching lobby opens. Never miss a legendary raid again.

Simple by design

Start Raiding in 3 Steps

No complicated setup. Open the app, create your trainer profile, and you're in the feed within 60 seconds.

1
Create Your Profile
Scan your trainer card or enter your name, team, level, and 12-digit friend code manually.
2
Browse or Host
Jump into the live lobby feed to find raids, or tap Host to open your own lobby in under 10 seconds.
3
Add & Battle
Share friend codes, add each other in-game, and start the raid together. Rate your host when you're done.
